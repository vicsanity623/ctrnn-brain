How to Play

  1. Diamonds ◆ spawn within a mile of you. Walk within 20 yards and tap one to collect it.
  2. Spend diamonds on the Earn wheel for a chance at Elden Bucks (EB).
  3. Zoom the map in close to reveal the 10ft tile grid. Tap an empty tile to buy it for 100 EB.
  4. Every tile you own earns EB automatically, every second, forever.
