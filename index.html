Survival Guide

🪓 1. Gathering & Companions

Walk near green Trees and gray Rocks and press Gather to harvest Wood and Stone. Your companion auto-attacks wild shadows!

Select Wall, Cook, or Base from the hotbar. Move your finger to aim the holographic green grid cursor and tap to place!

🍳 3. Super Berry Cooking

Build a Campfire and tap it to cook Super Berries. Feeding these to your companion permanently boosts stats by +5%!

🏛️ 4. Automated Base Workers

Build a PokeSquare Base and tap it to station benched party Pokémon. They passively farm wood, stone, and Oran berries 24/7!
